      Why do fresh curds squeak?

      Young curds have a springy protein network. It rubs against your teeth as you bite, making the famous squeak. As the curd ages and absorbs moisture, that structure softens and the squeak fades.

      Curd vs. cheese: what's the difference?

      Curds are the fresh solids formed when milk is cultured and coagulated. Press, salt, and age those curds and they become the cheese wheels and blocks you know.

      What makes a useful score?

      Judge the shell and the cheese separately. Crunch tracks the coating; pull tracks the warm cheese stretch. Seasoning and sauce should support the curd, not hide it.

      How should we pace six stops?

      Share one order at each stop, hydrate, and leave room for the finale. The 1.4-mile walk after Pilot Project is the reset. Keep the route in order so Café Benelux absorbs the 3-4 PM kitchen pause.
